# Boundaries

## In scope

- Public, responsive landing page for Davide's massage and holistic treatment studio.
- Warm beige/cream visual system with terracotta-brown text and amber accents.
- Scroll-linked decorative Flow Line, treatment card disclosure, and accessible booking/contact CTA.

## Out of scope for the base milestone

- Payment processing, customer accounts, medical claims, or appointment-management backend.
- Real WhatsApp/booking identifiers until the client provides them.
- Client portrait, testimonials, and treatment prices as factual production content until supplied.
- Heavy animation/UI dependencies.

## Data and privacy

The first contact form is presentation-only. A real provider endpoint must be configured and verified before collecting or transmitting personal data.
